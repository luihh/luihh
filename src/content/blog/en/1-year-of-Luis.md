It was 2 years and 11 days ago when I created the Luis bot. One day, out of boredom, I got the idea to make a Discord bot about a "Russian roulette". I made it and added the bot to a community I belong to.

Later I saw the fishing bot that [AREDL](https://aredl.net/) had (back then the bot was exclusive to that server), and I got the idea to make something similar for the little joke bot I had made. The community's users ended up LOVING it, and little by little I kept scaling the bot up more.

First came the **"Christmas Update"** with the `/vault` command, the leveling mechanic you already know with an exclusive badge and codes with Christmas badges every week. This was mainly a test to make Christmas a bit more dynamic on the server and see how the bot would do with some kind of "seasons".

Then came the **"Gambling Update"**; this is where **Lucky Blocks**, **Plinko**, the **Hall of Fame**, and a few other things were born.

After a while, I ran a poll in the community to find out if they wanted the bot to go public, since up until that point the bot couldn't be invited to other servers, and I got a unanimous "Yes" from the users. So I got to work and made a change to both the code and the content, completely.

I rewrote almost the entire bot from scratch so it could support more servers, and I changed and redid a lot of the bot's content. One example would be removing a `/shop` where you could buy badges related to inside jokes from the server, along with many other changes related to those same jokes.

And exactly 1 year ago, something happened that I never imagined would change my life: I officially released the bot!

![](/luis1yearmessage.png)

1 year ago this bot became a reality, and although its start was very slow at first — meaning not that many people were adding it to their servers — suddenly the bot had an incredible, exponential growth.

The bot reached 100 servers and then 200 before I had even finished processing that it had reached 100. Then it hit 300, and so on, until just yesterday it reached 700 servers :)

![](/luis700servers.png)

As of now, **+3300 users have used the bot**, including many people I admire a lot, well-known pro players and content creators. The bot has reached communities I never would have imagined it would reach. It's a bot recognized by a huge part of the Spanish-speaking community, and it has also spread within the English-speaking community; it has even reached many communities that have nothing to do with Geometry Dash XD

I can't express the happiness that all the growth the bot had in just 1 year brings me. I started programming when I was about 12 years old, precisely because I wanted to make Discord bots, and I always wanted to have a well-known bot, but I had never achieved anything great. Thanks to each and every one of you, I feel like I fulfilled that dream I had when I started all this, and it truly completely exceeded the expectations I had. I am eternally grateful to you all: to everyone who has fished, who has invited the bot to a server, who has recommended it and talked about it, and to those who have reached out to ask me or tell me something about the bot. I really thank you.

So far, **825,700 levels** have been fished. I'm really looking forward to seeing what number we reach next year :)
